# Demo-to-Research Claim Map

This map keeps the visual explainer subordinate to the research. Rows group related visible wording and state transitions; repeated step copy inherits the cited qualification for its claim group.

| Visible claim group | Demo location | Canonical research | Primary evidence | Required qualification |
