SWISS BANKING · ILLUSTRATIVE PRESENTATION

A deposit is already digital.
Tokenisation changes how it moves.

01

Ordinary deposit

Alice's CHF 1,000 is money she can spend, but legally it is an amount Bank A owes her.

02

Claim

The debtor matters. Alice has a claim on Bank A—not on the app, wallet or payment network.

03

CBS

The core banking system and general ledger record the account and the bank's liability.

04

Tokenisation

A token adds a programmable representation. Its contract decides whether it is the claim, a mirror or only an instruction.

The question is not “is there a token?” It is “what does the token represent, who owes it, and which record is authoritative?”

WHAT TO REMEMBER

The technology is visible. The liability design is decisive.

CHF 100One amount

Its legal and operational meaning changes with the product model.

01

Who owes it?

The token may be an instruction before it becomes a direct deposit claim.

02

Which record is final?

CBS remains authoritative for the payment-instruction model.

03

What settles?

Between banks, SIC moves central-bank money. The retail token does not enter the SNB.

04

What if it fails?

A safe design pauses value and repairs through explicit controlled actions.
